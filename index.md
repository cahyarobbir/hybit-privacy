# Privacy Policy for Hybit

**Effective date:** 2026-10-14
**App:** Hybit – Habit Tracker (`com.hybit.app`)
**Developer:** Cahya Robbi
**Contact:** cahyarobbir@gmail.com

Hybit is an offline-first habit tracker for Android. This policy explains what information the app handles and how.

## 1. Summary

- Your habits, check-ins and settings stay **on your device**. We do not run a server and we do not collect, upload or sell your habit data.
- We do not require an account, and we do not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, camera, microphone or files.
- The app shows ads through **Google AdMob**. The Google Mobile Ads SDK inside the app collects and shares certain device identifiers and usage data with Google to serve ads (see section 4). This is the only data the app collects or shares.
- We do not sell your personal data.

## 2. Information stored on your device

Hybit stores the following locally, in the app's private storage:

- Habit names, icons/colors, repeat schedules and archive status
- Daily completion records (dates you marked habits done)
- App preferences, such as onboarding status and the number of unlocked habit slots

This data is never sent to us or to any third party by the app. It is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its storage.

**Android backup:** The app allows Android's system backup. If you have Google backup enabled on your device, Android may include the app's data in your Google account backup. That is controlled by your device settings and Google's own privacy policy, not by us.

## 3. Information we do not collect

Other than the advertising data described in section 4, the app does not collect personal information, precise location, contacts, photos, files, audio, health data, or financial information. We have no analytics or crash-reporting service, and no data is sent to servers operated by us.

## 3a. Data summary

| Data | Collected | Shared | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| Habits, check-ins, settings | Stored on device only | No | App functionality |
| Advertising ID | Yes, by Google AdMob SDK | Yes, with Google | Advertising, fraud prevention |
| IP address, device and app info, ad interactions, diagnostics | Yes, by Google AdMob SDK | Yes, with Google | Advertising, analytics, fraud prevention |
| Name, email, phone, address, location, contacts, photos, files, health, financial data | No | No | n/a |

Data sent to Google is transmitted over the internet using encryption (HTTPS) where supported by the SDK.

## 4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Hybit uses Google AdMob to show banner ads and rewarded ads. Watching a rewarded ad unlocks one extra habit slot. AdMob is the only part of the app that uses the internet.

When ads are shown, Google and its partners may collect and process, for example:

- Advertising ID (when available and not reset or opted out by you)
- IP address and approximate region derived from it
- Device and app information (model, OS version, app version, language)
- Ad interaction and diagnostic data

Google uses this information to deliver, measure and personalize ads and to prevent fraud. The developer does not receive this data and does not control Google's use of it. See:

- Google's Privacy & Terms: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- How Google uses data from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
- AdMob and Firebase data disclosures: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

### Consent and your choices

- Where required (for example in the EEA, UK and some other regions), the app shows a consent form using Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) before ads are requested.
- You can change your choice at any time in **Settings → Privacy options**, which appears only where consent is required.
- You can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in your Android settings (**Settings → Privacy → Ads**).
- If you decline personalized ads, you may still see non-personalized ads.

## 5. Permissions

Hybit does not request any sensitive permissions. Through the Google Mobile Ads SDK, the app uses network access (to load ads) and, on supported Android versions, the advertising ID permission.

## 6. Children's privacy

Hybit is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through ads or otherwise, contact us and we will help address it.

## 7. Data retention and deletion

Because your habit data lives only on your device, you control it. To delete it, clear the app's storage or uninstall the app. Data held by Google (for example ad-related data or a device backup) is governed by Google's policies and your Google account settings.

## 8. Security

Your data stays in the app's private storage, protected by Android's app sandbox. No method of storage is perfectly secure, but since we do not transmit your habit data, the risk of it being exposed through our systems is minimal.

## 9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data. We do not hold your personal data, so there is nothing for us to access or delete on our side. For data processed by Google, use Google's privacy controls or contact Google directly.

## 10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version, with its effective date, will always be available at the link on the app's store listing. Material changes will be reflected by updating the effective date above.

## 11. Legal bases (EEA / UK)

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, advertising data is processed based on your **consent**, collected through the consent form shown in the app. You can withdraw consent at any time in **Settings → Privacy options**.
